# Cosmic Snake

A space-themed take on Snake, built as a single HTML file: no libraries, no build step.

**Play it:** https://liam6251.github.io/cosmic-snake/

## How to play
- Steer with the arrow keys or WASD. On a phone, swipe.
- Press Space (or the pause button) to pause.
- The edges are wormholes: go off one side and you come out the other.
- Your own tail is the only thing that can kill you.
- Eat stars for 10 points. Catch the golden supernova for 50 before it fades (about 5 seconds).
- The snake speeds up a little with every star. Your best score is saved in your browser.

## Features
- Glowing comet snake (cyan to violet to pink), twinkling starfield with parallax, nebula glow and shooting stars
- Particle bursts, wormhole swirls, screen shake and an explosion on death
- Synthesized sound effects (Web Audio) with reverb and echo, and a mute button
- Works on desktop and mobile

## Run it locally
Download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser.
